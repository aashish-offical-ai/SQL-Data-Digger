QUESTION 4:
Retrieve the top 3 most ordered products.
------------------------------------------------------------

QUERY:
SELECT
    ProductID,
    SUM(Quantity) AS Total_Quantity_Sold
FROM OrderDetails
GROUP BY ProductID
ORDER BY Total_Quantity_Sold DESC
LIMIT 3;

OUTPUT:

+-----------+--------------------+
| ProductID | Total_Quantity_Sold|
+-----------+--------------------+
| 204       | 3                  |
| 205       | 3                  |
| 201/202   | 1                  |
+-----------+--------------------+

REASON:

Product 204:
2 + 1 = 3 units sold

Product 205:
3 units sold

Product 201:
1 unit sold

Product 202:
1 unit sold

LIMIT 3 ki wajah se sirf 3 products show honge.

IMPORTANT:
Product 201 aur 202 dono ki quantity 1 hai, isliye tie hai.
ORDER BY mein tie-breaker nahi diya gaya hai. MySQL kisi ek ko
third position par return kar sakta hai.

Agar fixed result chahiye, query mein ProductID ko secondary sorting
ke liye add kar sakte hain.
